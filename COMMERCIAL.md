## Free

**Non-commercial use, including home use.** Your own computers, hobby projects,
learning, home labs. No limit on how many computers, and no time limit.

## Paid

**Commercial use** needs a paid license, priced per Computer per year. A Computer
is each physical or virtual machine the software runs on.

| Computers | Price per Computer / year | about per month |
|---|---|---|
| 1 - 10 | **$30** | $2.50 |
| 11 - 50 | **$24** | $2 |
| 51 - 250 | **$18** | $1.50 |
| 251+ | **$12** | $1 |

Your rate is set by the band your total Computer count falls in. Example: 20
Computers is 20 x $24 = **$480 per year**.

- Multi-year, or more than 1,000 Computers: ask for a quote.
- Nonprofit, education, and open-source projects: ask.

Paid Use renews each year at the price published here when the new year begins.
A price change applies only to periods that start after the change (License,
Section 8). How use made before paying is handled is set by Section 12 of the
License. This page does not change it.

## Questions people ask

**Is a home office a "home"?** If you use it for work you are paid for, that is
commercial use. If it is your own machine for your own purposes, it is free.

**Does it phone home?** No. The License does not allow the software to send usage
data for enforcement (Section 10). Usage statements come from local records and
only on a written request.

**Is this open source?** No. It is source-available: read it, run it, and use it
non-commercially for free. Redistribution is reserved (License, Section 2).
